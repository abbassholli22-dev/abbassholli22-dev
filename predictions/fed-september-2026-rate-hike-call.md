My base-case prediction is that the **Federal Reserve will raise the federal funds rate by 25 basis points (+25bp)** at the September 2026 FOMC meeting.

## Rationale

The prediction is based on the following macro conditions:

- Core inflation remains above the Federal Reserve’s 2% target.
- The labor market remains resilient enough to allow restrictive policy to continue.
- Higher oil prices create renewed inflation risk through transportation and production costs.
- A +25bp hike is consistent with the base case in the accompanying cross-asset scenario analysis.

## Market interpretation

The rate increase itself may be largely priced in. The larger market reaction is likely to depend on the **dot plot, inflation and growth projections, and Chair Powell’s guidance** about future policy.

A hawkish message could support the US dollar and Treasury yields while weighing on gold and Bitcoin. A dovish signal that the hike is the last of the cycle could produce the opposite reaction despite the hike being delivered.
